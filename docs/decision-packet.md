## Current implementation boundary

The Angular root is ready for engineering smoke play, not final product acceptance. The current fixed-room implementation is at `09430519b1b3f2ccb6143d232bc3f07088af0104`: Node 24.19.0/npm 11.17.0, Biome formatting/linting, 130 unit/component tests, 29 Chromium journeys, and a clean production build. The progression boundary rejects overflow-sized victory experience, malformed boss progress, and a boss reward that would exceed the three-boss schedule before state publication; the persistence boundary rejects orphaned combat damage and malformed optional fields; and the combat boundaries reject unknown damage kinds and actions instead of falling through to Magic. The automated accessibility contract exposes labeled groups for champion selection, combat actions, power-up choices, and store offers.

The green baseline proves reproducibility and safety boundaries. D-001 and the prototype portion of D-002 are resolved, while D-003 through D-008 and the remaining final acceptance reviews remain open. A future agent must not mark the rewrite complete while any open owner decision or acceptance review remains.

## Finalization sequence

1. Review the accepted D-001 and D-002 baselines together during manual combat and route play.
2. Resolve D-003 and D-008 before retaining or expanding persistence or introducing any service boundary.
3. Complete D-004 and D-005 before publishing the media-heavy UI.
4. Resolve D-006 and D-007 before promoting a release candidate.
5. Record accepted outcomes as ADRs, update the affected characterization fixtures, rerun `npm run verify:release`, and only then schedule owner manual play as final acceptance.

Until then, unresolved items remain visible in [TODO.md](TODO.md), and agents should continue safe characterization work without silently selecting among the alternatives.
