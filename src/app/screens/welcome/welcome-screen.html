How to play

Field manual

Choose a champion, read the enemy, and make each turn count. Your run ends when your reaches zero.

Make your move

When it is your turn, choose the tool that matches the enemy’s weakness.

Attack deals physical damage, checked against Armor.
Magic deals magic damage, checked against Magic resist.
Potion restores health, but gives the enemy the next turn.
Guard gains temporary Block, which absorbs incoming damage until your next turn.

Enemy intents preview the next action and show damage per hit. Block absorbs each hit until your next turn. A Magic hit applies an Arcane mark, making your next Attack deal 25% more damage. Venom damages an enemy at the start of its turn.

Choose a route

Skirmish · A standard fight for experience and gold.

Elite · A tougher fight with a better reward.

Merchant · Buy potions and passive relics.

Campfire · Recover some Health before moving on.

Boss · Face a guardian at rooms 10, 20, and 30.

Read the battlefield

Every icon has a job. The same colors and symbols follow you into combat.

Health is your life total. Reach zero and the run is over.
Attack powers physical strikes.
Magic powers arcane strikes.
Armor reduces physical damage received.
Magic resist reduces magic damage received.
Speed decides who opens the encounter.

Build the run

  1. Find an enemy. Compare speed, then trade turns until someone falls.
  2. Claim the victory. Earn experience and gold; level-ups improve your stats.
  3. Choose your power-up. Pending upgrades must be selected before the next encounter.
  4. Spend with intent. The store sells repeatable potions and one-time equipment.

Survive deeper floors, defeat the guardians at milestone rooms, and leave a record on the wall.
